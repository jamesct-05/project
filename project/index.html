Boat Water Sports

Boat water sports are water-based recreational activities performed behind a boat or watercraft. This page will focus the tow-sports of tubing, wakeboarding, water skiing, and kneeboarding. The rider is on some sort of device behind the boat and pulled using an attached rope.

Each sport uses a different way to ride behind the boat. Tubing is done on an inflatable tube, while water skiing uses one or two skis. Kneeboarding is done on a small board while the rider is close to the water, and wakeboarding uses a wider board that the rider stands on.

These sports can be fun for people with different levels of experience, but it is important to stay safe. Riders should wear a life jacket and listen to the driver, who should watch the rider and avoid other boats and obstacles.
