Kneeboarding

Kneeboarding is a water sport where a rider kneels on a small, padded board while being towed behind a boat. Fins on the board help with stability and turning.

To get started, lie on your stomach on the board and hold the rope or attach it to the board's starting hook. As the boat pulls you forward, bring your knees onto the pad and secure the strap.

Keep these basics in mind for a safe ride:

  1. Wear a properly fitted life jacket and ride with an experienced driver and spotter.
  2. Choose clear water away from swimmers, docks, and other boats.
  3. Start at a comfortable speed; the guide suggests around 15–20 mph and adjusting to the rider.

When choosing a kneeboard, look for one that suits your size and experience. A padded knee area, secure strap, and stable design can make it more comfortable for beginners.

Once you are comfortable riding, you can try simple tricks such as wake jumps, spins, and tail grabs. Take your time learning, follow local boating rules, and keep the rider clear of the boat's propeller.
